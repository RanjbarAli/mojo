About Mojo

Built around horse welfare, great coaching and a love of the sport.

Our story

Riding, done properly

Mojo began with a simple idea: give riders in Oman a place to learn properly — on happy horses, with coaches who've competed at the highest level.

Today we teach show jumping, dressage and riding school lessons for every level, start young horses, run a leasing programme and host national and international competitions. Between rides, our café is the place to watch the action.
